# Source allowlist

Preferred sources are `whitehouse.gov`, `omb.gov`, `cisa.gov`, `dhs.gov`, `nist.gov`, `csrc.nist.gov`, and `federalregister.gov`. Other `.gov` hosts may be accepted when they are the issuing or official archival authority. `doi.org` is accepted only for NIST publication DOI links.

Search pages, blogs, vendor summaries, mirrors, link shorteners, and unofficial archives are not record sources. An official press release may support discovery but should not replace a live directive or publication page when the instrument itself is available.
